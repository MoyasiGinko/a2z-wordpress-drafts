002 Founder Story
Section 002 / 006

A discipline learned somewhere different.

The Founder's Background

Before A2Z, there was Subsea 7 — and the relentless rigour of Big 4 audit standards applied to complex group accounts in the North Sea energy sector. That experience built the firm you see today.

The North Sea

It began deep beneath the North Sea at Subsea 7, where Shabbir Rahman managed complex group accounts and operated to the full rigour of Big 4 audit standards — Deloitte and EY scrutinising every number, every quarter, without exception.

The Discipline

Those years demanded bulletproof processes, precise systems, and zero tolerance for error. Every reconciliation and group consolidation had to hold under scrutiny from the most rigorous reviewers in the profession.

The Decision — 2018

When A2Z was founded in 2018, that discipline was not aspirational — it was operational from the first engagement letter. Specialist departments rather than generalists. Multiple independent quality-control layers rather than a single reviewer.

The Result

One hundred per cent paperless, proactively managed, and delivered to the standards Shabbir applied at corporate level — only now to the clients who had previously been priced out of them.

003 Three Principles
Section 003 / 006

Three positions. Non-negotiable.

Every engagement is built on three positions that never move. They are not marketing language — they define how this firm operates, and what every client can expect without asking.

I

We do not
compete
on price.

Cheap accounting creates expensive problems. Missed deadlines, incorrect filings, reactive advice — these carry real costs that dwarf any fee saving. We price for the value delivered: certainty, accuracy, and strategic advantage that compounds over time.

Protects: Long-term value
II

We do not
sell
hours.

Hourly billing creates misaligned incentives — it rewards slow work and penalises clients for asking questions. Every engagement is scoped for specific, guaranteed outcomes: fixed-fee, fully defined, and confirmed in writing before any work begins.

Protects: Cost certainty
III

We do not
react to
problems.

Reactive accounting is not accounting — it is damage control. The four-layer quality structure identifies and resolves issues long before they reach the client or HMRC. Every deadline is met in advance. Every risk is surfaced early.

Protects: Compliance & peace of mind

005 Three Departments
Section 005 / 006

Specialist departments. One point of contact.

Generalist firms ask one accountant to handle everything. We separate the disciplines so each area is handled by someone whose entire professional focus is that specific work — and nothing else.

01

Payroll & Billing

Full-service payroll and credit control — RTI, PAYE, pension auto-enrolment, and accounts receivable managed end to end.

  • RTI submissions and PAYE management
  • Pension auto-enrolment administration
  • Credit control and aged-debtor reporting
  • P11D benefits-in-kind processing
  • Statutory sick pay and maternity calculations
  • HMRC correspondence and resolution
02

VAT & Management Accounts

MTD-compliant bookkeeping, quarterly VAT returns, and regular management reporting — delivered in real time, with commentary.

  • Monthly bookkeeping and reconciliation
  • Quarterly VAT returns (MTD-compliant)
  • Management accounts with commentary
  • Cash-flow forecasting and reporting
  • VAT scheme selection and optimisation
  • Making Tax Digital compliance
03

Year-End & Tax

Annual accounts, Corporation Tax, self-assessment, and year-round strategic planning — not just compliance, but active tax management.

  • Statutory annual accounts preparation
  • CT600 Corporation Tax returns

  • Companies House filing and compliance
  • Research & Development tax credit claims
