It began deep beneath the North Sea at Subsea 7, where Shabbir managed complex group accounts and operated to the full rigour of Big 4 audit standards — Deloitte and EY scrutinising every number, every quarter, without exception.

Those years demanded bulletproof processes, precise systems, and zero tolerance for error. Every reconciliation, every group consolidation, every audit deliverable had to hold under scrutiny from the most rigorous reviewers in the profession.

When A2Z was founded in 2018, that discipline became the foundation — not aspirational, but operational from the first engagement letter. Specialist departments, multiple independent quality-control layers, and FCCA director-level oversight on every single engagement.

100% paperless, proactively managed, delivered to the standards applied at corporate level — only now to the clients who had previously been priced out of them. Every engagement carries three written guarantees — contractual commitments, because you deserve to know exactly what you are buying.

“We didn’t build another accounting firm. We built the finance function that growing businesses deserved — and never had access to.”
