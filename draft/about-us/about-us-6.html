It began deep beneath the North Sea at Subsea 7, where Shabbir Rahman managed complex group accounts and operated to the full rigour of Big 4 audit standards — Deloitte and EY scrutinising every number, every quarter, without exception.

Those years demanded bulletproof processes, precise systems, and zero tolerance for error. When A2Z was founded in 2018, that discipline was not aspirational — it was operational from the first engagement letter.

A2Z was built on an entirely different model: specialist departments rather than generalists, multiple independent quality-control layers rather than a single reviewer, and FCCA director-level oversight on every single client engagement.

One hundred per cent paperless, proactively managed, and delivered to the standards Shabbir applied at corporate level — only now to the clients who had previously been priced out of them.

Three things we will not compromise.

Every engagement is built on three non-negotiable positions. They are not marketing — they define how the firm works, and what every client may expect.

i

We do not compete on price.

ii

We do not sell hours.

Every engagement is for specific, guaranteed outcomes — fixed-fee, fully scoped, and confirmed in writing before any work begins.

iii

We do not react to problems.

Four layers of review. Every single time.

No output leaves A2Z without passing through every layer. Not a checklist — a structural guarantee, built into how the firm operates at every client tier.

i

ii

The department head independently reviews for technical accuracy, compliance, and completeness.

iii

iv
