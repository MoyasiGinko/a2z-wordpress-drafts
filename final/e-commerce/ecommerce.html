THE REAL PROBLEMS

Where online sellers

How we stop it

Profit hidden in fees and refunds

THE ISSUE

Marketplaces bundle commission, FBA charges, advertising and refunds into one payout. Booked as a single deposit, your real costs disappear.

HOW WE FIX IT

We split every fee, refund and chargeback line by line, so your accounts and VAT return reflect only the genuine costs you incurred.

VAT that varies by border

THE ISSUE

Distance-selling thresholds, OSS and IOSS, import VAT and customs duties differ by country, product and shipping method, and the rules keep moving.

HOW WE FIX IT

We register and file correctly across the UK and EU, and document the treatment for HMRC, so you neither overpay nor get caught out later.

Cash trapped in stock

THE ISSUE

Overstock ties up cash, understock loses sales, and a healthy profit on paper can still leave you short when it is time to reorder.

HOW WE FIX IT

Cash-flow forecasting that links stock to cash, so you grow and reorder with confidence rather than crossing your fingers.

Selling well, but somehow broke

THE ISSUE

High turnover does not mean high profit. Without margin by product and channel, it is easy to scale the very things that quietly lose money.

HOW WE FIX IT

Clear margin per product and per channel, so you cut what loses, back what works, and scale on evidence.

QUESTIONS WE ARE ASKED EVERY DAY

E-commerce accounting,
Answered plainly

Do I really need a specialist e-commerce accountant? +

If you sell across multiple platforms or internationally, yes. Online accounting is more complex than traditional bookkeeping, mainly because of marketplace fees and cross-border VAT. A specialist gets the margin and VAT right, which is usually worth far more than the fee.

Why does my store feel busy but not very profitable? +

Almost always because fees, refunds, VAT and stock sit between turnover and profit. Once we reconcile each channel and show margin per product, the picture becomes clear, and so do the changes that lift profit.

How do you handle VAT for online sellers? +

We manage UK VAT, EU VAT through OSS and IOSS, import VAT, distance selling and the margin scheme, all under Making Tax Digital. We set the treatment up correctly, file each return, and keep records that stand up if HMRC ever asks.

Can you track profit across Amazon and Shopify together? +

Yes. We reconcile every platform, fee and payout, then report margin by channel and by product, so you see your true profit across the whole business rather than per dashboard.

Do you only work with online sellers in your area? +

No. We are an e-commerce specialist serving online businesses across the UK. Our work is fully cloud-based, so where you are based makes no difference to the service you receive.
