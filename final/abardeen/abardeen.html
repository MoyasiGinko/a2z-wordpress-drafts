THE RIGHT FIT

done properly

We do our best work for established, owner-managed businesses that want a proactive accountant, not just a filing service. In fairness to you, here is who we suit - and who we honestly do not.

A strong fit

  • Established service or consulting businesses (typically £500k-£5m turnover).

  • Owners looking to scale, exit, or step back from day-to-day operations.

  • You value strategic advice over just getting the cheapest tax return filed.

  • You are willing to use modern cloud software (Xero) to stay organised.

  • You appreciate responsiveness and want a partner, not a transaction.

Probably not for you

  • Startups pre-revenue or sole traders looking for basic year-end compliance only.

  • Retail, hospitality, or heavy manufacturing businesses (we stick to our niche).

  • You see accounting strictly as a necessary evil to minimize at all costs.

We are happy to recommend excellent firms if you fall into these categories.
